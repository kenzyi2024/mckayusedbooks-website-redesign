About

A second life for good stories

Since 1983, Manassas's place to buy, sell and trade the books and media people love — one big room, endlessly restocked by the community that shops it.

The book you've finished is the book someone else is looking for.

That simple, generous idea is the whole engine. Bring in what you're done with, and it becomes credit toward your next discovery. Multiply it by forty years and a whole community, and you get a store with hundreds of thousands of items and a genuinely unpredictable inventory.

The shelves are famously sorted by color — a browsing experience closer to a treasure hunt than a search bar. It's why people come in for one thing and leave with an armful, and why McKay has never really needed an online catalog: the finding is the fun.

Help & FAQ

Questions, answered once

Everything the old site split across two overlapping FAQ pages, merged and sorted into three plain sections.

Shopping
Do you sell online?+

No — McKay's used inventory is in-store only. The fun (and the honesty) of a store this size is that the shelves change by the hour. For new books, McKay has a Bookshop.org affiliate that ships, but that's not McKay's used stock.

Can you check whether a specific title is in stock?+

There's no live catalog to search, so we can't guarantee a title is on the shelf at a given moment. Call the store or come browse — with hundreds of thousands of items turning over daily, an online list would be out of date immediately.

What do you carry?+

Books of every kind, movies (DVD, Blu-ray, some VHS), music (CDs and vinyl), video games and consoles, tabletop and RPG games, plus puzzles, Legos, action figures and more. See What We Sell.

Trading
How does trade credit work?+

Bring in items you're done with and we turn what we can use into store credit. Two kinds: blue trade (earned on books and VHS, buys books) and white trade (earned on DVDs, CDs and audiobooks, buys anything). See Sell & Trade.

What can I bring in?+

Books, movies, music, video games (and consoles), tabletop games and puzzles are all fair game. Some items we can't take — heavily damaged copies, ex-library books, and certain editions. to confirm the exact list; when in doubt, bring it and ask.

Do I need an appointment to trade?+

No appointment for a normal trade — bring your box during trade hours (9 AM–7 PM daily), hand it to Check-In, and shop while we sort. For very large loads it helps to give the store a heads-up. to confirm
